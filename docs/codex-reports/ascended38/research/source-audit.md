# Source audit — Island Survival Guide
Reviewed 2026-10-08. Route: 8 phases / 87 goals. Milestone sizes, optional leisure tasks, phase order and shared army quantities are editorial planning choices. They are not presented as mandatory game unlocks or guaranteed victory thresholds.

## Player evidence read
- Smilloww's PvE Island game plan and comment replies: starter camp, Moschops versus Parasaur, utility progression and optional creative play. Removed dangerous water-escape advice and the outdated claim that herbivores halve percent-health Dragon burn.
- First-playthrough ASA discussion: flyer/gatherer/crafting progression, Baryonyx and ocean cave preparation.
- Small-tribe Island discussion: ocean/exploration as part of progression, cakes and mixed-use tames rather than exclusively breeding armies.
- Solo "Easiest Boss" discussion: combat difficulty differs from artifact-access difficulty; first two guardians may be swapped.
- Current ASA boss-team discussion: shared Therizino/Yuty route remains one player choice among many. Recent Alpha warnings contradict mechanical reuse of old 21k-HP advice.
- BEYPlaysGames paste footage previously reviewed for build 37: mounted frog farming in Swamp Cave. Reuses verified knowledge without new media capture.

## Mechanics cross-checks
ARK Wiki search/page excerpts were read for Broodmother, Megapithecus, Dragon, Breeding, Imprinting, Tek Cave, Overseer, Deinosuchus, Basilosaurus and Caves. Beacon's ASA recipe page was checked for cake inputs. Some Wiki direct page opens returned Internal Error; indexed source excerpts were used for those claims and limitations remain explicit. Breeding/Mutations pages flag ASA update gaps, so the guide imports basic parent-stat inheritance and imprint distinction, without mutation probabilities, imposed waiting times or server configuration.

- Guardian artifact sets are explicit and matched to the Island's existing catalogue.
- Standard arena team cap is 20: 19 fighters + ridden Yuty is a suggested full roster; optional Daeodon replaces a fighter, not an extra 21st animal.
- Cakes heal eligible herbivores; they do not cancel percent-health fire breath. No blanket Alpha-ready HP/melee/saddle threshold imported.
- AI offspring retain imprint stat benefits, but the entire AI army is not assumed to receive the rider-only imprint bonus.
- Patch 73.13 removes Deinosuchus boss bleed; no older bleed shortcut promoted.
- Gamma Tek Cave gate: three guardian Gamma trophies (or higher equivalents), level 60. Higher tiers need their matching tributes; no cryopod-in-cave assumption or uncertain cave-tame cap imported.
- Guide contains no copied coordinates. Existing map-specific Atlas/cave records provide location reference.
